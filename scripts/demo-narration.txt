Map Jinn turns real building footprints into printable map artwork. The demo starts with the built-in account flow, then loads the live GIS workspace. Search a ZIP code such as 30331 and Map Jinn moves into that footprint area instead of merely typing the query. Switch between filled and outline footprint styles, add a custom apparel label and live coordinates, and preview the artwork in dark or paper mode. Focus mode gives the map more room on screen. When the design is ready, export a print-ready PNG directly from the rendered map.
